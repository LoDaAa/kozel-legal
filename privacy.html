Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 10, 2026 · Last updated: September 10, 2026

Summary: Közel is a local community service. Depending on the features you use, we process account, authentication, profile, location, community-content, notification, technical and security-related information to provide the service, keep it relevant to your area and protect the community.

1. What We Process

Depending on the features you use, Közel may process your phone number, account identifier, display name, profile photo if you provide one, reports, photographs, comments, replies, reactions, notification preferences, technical information and security-related signals.

Community activity can include the content you submit, the time it was created, relevant report information and interactions with that content.

2. Location Information

Certain Közel features require location information to provide local functionality, determine relevant community content, support neighborhood features and help prevent abuse. Location information is handled for defined purposes and only to the extent reasonably necessary for the relevant feature or security purpose.

You can control location permissions through iOS settings. Some features may not work correctly when the relevant permission is disabled.

3. Authentication

Közel uses phone-number verification to create, authenticate and protect accounts. Verification codes are not displayed publicly to other users.

4. Community Content

Reports, photographs, comments, replies and reactions are part of Közel's community functionality. Content submitted to public or community-facing features may be visible to other users as part of the service.

Users are responsible for the content they submit and should not publish another person's private or sensitive information without a lawful basis.

5. Why We Use Information

Information may be processed to provide and improve the service, authenticate accounts, operate community features, provide relevant local functionality, deliver notifications, maintain security, prevent fraud and abuse, communicate with users and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Depending on the processing activity, the applicable legal basis may include performance of a contract, legal obligation, legitimate interests, or consent where required.

6. Service Providers

Közel may use technical service providers required to operate the application, including authentication, database, cloud, notification, mapping and diagnostic infrastructure. Such providers may process information on behalf of Közel as necessary to provide their services.

The specific production services used by the application may change as the service develops. Where required, applicable provider and data-processing information will be reflected in this policy.

7. Notifications

If notifications are enabled, Közel may process technical notification identifiers and your selected notification preferences. Notifications may relate to community activity and relevant local reports. You can control notification permissions through the app and your device settings.

8. Security and Trust

Közel may use technical and account-related signals to detect abuse, fraudulent activity, manipulation and other security risks. Trust-related indicators are service and security signals; they are not official identity verification or legal classifications.

9. Retention and Deletion

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose, applicable legal obligations, security requirements or the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

You may request deletion of your Közel account through the Profile and Account section of the app or through the Delete Account Request page.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law, for security purposes, or for legal claims.

10. Your Rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable data-protection law, you may have rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection. You may also have the right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Privacy requests can be submitted to kozelbudakeszi@gmail.com.

11. Children's Privacy

Közel is not directed to children under the minimum age permitted by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information in circumstances where this is not permitted, contact us.

12. International Processing

Depending on the service providers used to operate Közel, information may be processed in countries other than your country of residence. Where required, appropriate safeguards will be used for international transfers.

13. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Közel develops or legal requirements change. Material changes will be communicated where appropriate. The effective date above identifies the current version.

14. Supervisory Authority

The Hungarian supervisory authority for data protection is the National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH). You may contact the competent supervisory authority where you believe your personal information has been processed contrary to applicable data-protection law.

15. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact kozelbudakeszi@gmail.com.
