1. About Közel

Közel is a local community service that helps people discover, share and discuss information relevant to their area. The service may include community reports, maps, notifications, comments, replies, reactions and other local features.

Important: Közel is not an emergency service, police service, fire service, ambulance service, government authority or official traffic-management system. In an emergency or when official assistance is required, contact the appropriate emergency or public authority.

2. Accounts

Közel uses phone-number verification to help create and protect user accounts. You are responsible for maintaining access to your account and for activity carried out through it.

You must not impersonate another person, create fraudulent accounts, abuse another person's account, or attempt to bypass security controls.

3. Community Content

Users are responsible for reports, photographs, comments, replies and other content they submit.

Content must not be unlawful, threatening, abusive, harassing, hateful, fraudulent, deliberately misleading or invasive of another person's privacy. False emergencies, deliberately dangerous misinformation and content intended to cause panic are prohibited.

4. Respecting Other People

Do not publish another person's private information without a lawful basis. Do not publish private phone numbers, exact residential information, identity documents or other sensitive information about another person.

Users should respect applicable privacy, image and personality rights when submitting content.

5. Reports and Information

Közel displays information submitted by users and does not guarantee that every report is accurate, complete, current or officially verified. Users should independently assess information where circumstances require it.

Do not rely on Közel as a substitute for emergency services, official government information, professional advice or other appropriate authorities.

6. Community Safety and Moderation

Közel may review, restrict, label or remove content that violates applicable law, these Terms or community-safety rules. Accounts or features may be restricted where reasonably necessary to protect users, prevent abuse or preserve the integrity of the service.

7. Notifications

Közel may provide optional notifications relating to community activity and relevant local reports. Notification availability and delivery may depend on your settings, network conditions and the operating system.

8. Intellectual Property

Közel and its software, branding, design and original service materials are protected by applicable intellectual-property laws. You may not copy, modify, distribute, reverse engineer or commercially exploit the service except where permitted by law or with appropriate authorization.

By submitting content, you retain rights you already have in that content, while granting Közel the permissions reasonably necessary to host, store, reproduce, display and distribute that content as part of operating the service.

9. Prohibited Use

You must not use Közel to violate applicable law, harass or threaten others, distribute malicious software, interfere with the service, manipulate community signals, create fraudulent accounts, scrape or systematically collect information without authorization, or otherwise abuse the service.

10. Availability and Changes

Közel may change, suspend or discontinue features as the service develops. We do not guarantee that every feature will always be available or uninterrupted.

11. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your Közel account through the Profile and Account section of the app or through the Delete Account Request page. Some information may need to be retained where required by law, for security purposes or for legal claims.

These Terms may be updated as Közel develops. Where appropriate, users will receive notice of material changes. Continued use after an effective update constitutes acceptance of the updated Terms to the extent permitted by applicable law.
