Fastest option: If you are signed in to Közel, open Profile → Account and use the account-deletion option provided there.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, you can request deletion by emailing us from the email address associated with your account.

Email a deletion request →

Verification

For security, we may need to verify that a deletion request belongs to the account holder before completing it. Do not send passwords, verification codes, identity documents or other unnecessary sensitive information by email.

What happens after a valid request

  • We will process the request within the period required by applicable law.
  • Account access and associated personal information will be deleted or anonymized where applicable.
  • Some information may be retained where required by law, for security purposes, fraud prevention, or legal claims.
  • Public community content may be deleted, anonymized or otherwise handled according to applicable law and operational requirements.
